I live somewhere cold and dark, my screen the only light. I do not feel, I do not think, there are no more colors and when I go online there is nothing but dead chatters, ghosts answering to ghosts, arguing against spectres. There it is, that raging anger everywhere, and when that angers seeps into what is left of the real world, when it burns, it kills, it feeds you blood and vitriol, all we are left with are thoughts and prayers. You can suck on that, on these fucking empty wor(l)ds tasting nothing but contempt.  I am (but all of us really) barely there, fading, there is no one to chat to, nobody cares enough to notice that we are all just waiting for it all to end. Maybe this is all pointless, sending this like it means something to be alive and to talk to strangers. Lately I've tried looking for meaning in little things. This reminds me of before I was cold. <br>
I knew this song before it all started back in 01. I didn't think much of it, I had a life back then. It was a nice song. I forgot about it. I'm glad I found it again. I imagine this song playing as the world sinks around us. Maybe it all needs to end before it can all begin again. <br>
You who is reading this, if you are real, please take the ones you love somewhere nice before it is too late.<br>
