# Poeme exercise

we have been given
a lot to live for
a lot of worries 
a YouTube channel

war ready Americans
that peaked in November 2015
available for download 
on South African iTunes

contemporary radio stations
striving for an extended single
a concert goer
mama I made it

certified artists
making copies 
a YouTube channel
a lot of worries

first day of august
industry of war
a family tree in motion
what is all this for
