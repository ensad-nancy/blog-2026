# About me

I’m in my late twenties and live in a lively city. I work as a translator, but I’m not sure it’s the right path for me. I know lots of people, though I’m only really close to one childhood friend. I’m creative and nostalgic: I collect things, make collages and write stories that mix memories, reality and fiction as I try to understand myself.
