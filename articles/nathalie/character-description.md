# About me

I’m in my late twenties and live in a lively city, where there’s always something happening around me. I work as a translator, but I’m not sure whether this is really the path I want to follow. I know a lot of people, yet I feel truly close to only one person: my childhood friend. I’m creative and nostalgic, and I often find myself thinking about the past and the small details other people might overlook. I collect objects that mean something to me, make collages and write stories. In my work, memories, reality and fiction often blend together. Maybe I’m trying to make sense of my life and understand who I am.
