# Lights

<div class="poem">

October comes

Can’t find somebody

Car lights come…

Exciting, sexy, fun –

<div class="lust">lust</div>

… and goes.

One-night stands.



November comes

Somebody different

Completely different

Car lights come…

Careful, smooth, kind –

<div class="love">love</div>

… and goes.

Lovelorn.



October, November,

Exciting, Careful,

Sexy, Smooth,

Fun, Kind,


One-night stands,

Lovelorn.


Goes and goes.



Can’t find somebody completely different.



Acteur, Stories,

Stories, Actress,

Acteur, Stories,

Stories, Actress.


<div class="lights-small">Lights.</div>

Comes and goes.

</div>
