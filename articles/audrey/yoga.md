# didn't found peace

It’s been 4 months since this yoga mat has entered my home. This purchase was made in hopes of finding a moment of calm and relief after long days of being pushed to the edge by the harsh reality of a world darkened by capitalism, patriarchy, and state fascism. 

Let me be honest with you: no, you won’t find that kind of peace sitting cross-legged with your eyes closed at 2a.m., a random youtube meditation podcast in the back. 

However, it proves to be remarkably useful during a long week of music festival camping, to start a group stretching session to reconnect with the body that you physically weakened to the beat of the music, dancing on a dirt ground, at an hour far too late for what your back and knees could endure.  

All in all, the price seems fair, hoping that it retains its flexibility for when it sees the light of day again in ten months (and i’m not just talking about the mat). In any case, let’s pray it doesn’t have to be out of the dust before then, because with gas at 2.50€ per liter, it might end up out on the street sooner than predicted, and, once again, I'm not just talking about the mat.
